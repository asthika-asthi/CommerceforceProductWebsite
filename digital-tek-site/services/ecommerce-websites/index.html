What a shop from us includes

  • Product pages that show what you sell clearly, on phones as well as computers.
  • Basket and checkout that are simple to use, so fewer customers give up part-way.
  • Payments and delivery set-up, so you can take orders and ship them.
  • Search-friendly foundations: clear page titles and descriptions, a clean structure, fast loading and product markup that helps Google understand what you sell.
  • Handover: you learn how to add products and manage orders yourself, and we stay around when you need us.

The exact list is set out in your written quote, so you know what you are getting before anything starts.

How it works

  1. Free chat. You tell us what you sell, who buys it and how you want to run the shop.

  3. Build and review. We build it and show you progress, and you tell us what to change.
