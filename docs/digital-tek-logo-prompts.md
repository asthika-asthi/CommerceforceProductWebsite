# Digital-tek Logo Generation Prompts

Five logo concepts to try in an AI image tool, with instructions for using them and choosing one. The colours match the Digital-tek website: cream `#FBF6EE`, ink `#1A1F2E`, peach `#F4C9B8` / `#E8A598`, sky `#B8D4E0`, mint `#C8DCC8`, sand `#E8D4B8`, lilac `#D9CFE8`. The website fonts are Instrument Serif and DM Sans.

## How to use these prompts

1. Pick a concept and copy its prompt.
2. Paste the **Shared spec block** after it, in the same message.
3. If your tool has a negative-prompt field, paste the **Negative prompt** there. Otherwise add it to the end as "Avoid: ...".
4. Generate 4 to 8 variations of each concept and keep the best one or two.
5. **Lettering:** AI image tools often misspell or distort text.
   - Generate the symbol alone first, using the main prompt without the "With text" line.
   - Add "Digital-tek" yourself in Canva or Figma, using Instrument Serif or DM Sans.
   - Use the "With text" line only if you want the tool to draw the lettering. Check the spelling carefully.
6. Run the choosing tests below, then the trademark check.

## Shared spec block

Paste this at the end of every prompt:

> **Output:** flat vector-style logo, perfectly centred on a plain solid #FBF6EE background, square 1:1 composition, 4096×4096 px, ultra-sharp clean edges, crisp geometric precision, no gradients unless stated, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup, no photograph, no watermark, no extra text, generous empty margin (at least 20% on each side). Must stay recognisable at 32×32 px favicon size and in a single colour (black or white).

## Negative prompt

> blurry, low resolution, jpeg artifacts, photorealistic, 3D render, glossy, bevel, drop shadow, clutter, tiny details, busy background, random letters, misspelled text, gibberish text, cartoon mascot, stock clipart, circuit-board cliché, binary code rain, neon cyberpunk, robot head

## Concept 1: "The Lowercase d" (monogram)

Best for a favicon, app icon and social avatar. It builds on the "d" already used on the website.

> A minimalist monogram logo of a lowercase letter "d" for a UK digital studio called Digital-tek. The bowl of the "d" is a perfect circle and the stem is a straight vertical line, and where they meet a small notch suggests a pixel or a cursor. Italic editorial serif character, like Instrument Serif. Colours: ink navy #1A1F2E for the letter, with the circle filled in soft peach #F4C9B8 and a thin 3 px ink outline. Friendly, warm, confident, modern. Sits inside a rounded square with 22% corner radius for an app-icon version. Simple geometry, balanced negative space, a single idea, timeless.

**With text:** add "Beside the monogram, the wordmark 'Digital-tek' in an elegant italic serif, ink #1A1F2E, with a small hyphen detail."

## Concept 2: "Connected Dots" (automation and connection)

Reflects AI automation, integrations and linking your tools.

> A modern abstract logo symbol for Digital-tek, a company that connects businesses' tools with AI automation. Three solid circles in peach #E8A598, sky #B8D4E0 and mint #C8DCC8, joined by two smooth rounded lines that form a soft, flowing "d" or an upward-right arrow. Evenly weighted, thick rounded strokes, a hand-drawn warmth in the geometry, but crisp and precise. Ink navy #1A1F2E for the connecting lines. Feels approachable and intelligent, not corporate-cold and not sci-fi. Plenty of whitespace. Works in one colour.

**With text:** add "Wordmark 'Digital-tek' to the right in lowercase DM Sans medium, letter-spacing +2%, ink #1A1F2E."

## Concept 3: "Pixel to Page" (web and design)

Reflects websites and design and creative: the shift from pixels to a finished layout.

> A clean geometric logo mark for Digital-tek, a UK web, design and AI studio. A 3×3 grid of rounded squares where the squares gradually become one solid shape. Top-left squares are small and separate, and bottom-right squares merge into a single bold rounded rectangle, suggesting pixels turning into a finished page. Palette: sand #E8D4B8, peach #F4C9B8, sky #B8D4E0 and lilac #D9CFE8 for the squares, with the largest merged shape in ink #1A1F2E. Flat design, soft rounded corners (radius 18%), a balanced and calm composition, editorial and warm. High contrast between the dark merged shape and the pastel pieces.

**With text:** add "Wordmark 'Digital-tek' under the mark, centred, in a refined serif with an italic 'tek'."

## Concept 4: "The Spark Wordmark" (type-led)

A typographic logo, best for the website header and documents.

> A sophisticated type-only logo of the words "Digital-tek" for a boutique UK digital studio. Elegant high-contrast display serif in the style of Instrument Serif, "Digital" in upright ink navy #1A1F2E and "tek" in italic, joined by a short, thin hyphen. A small four-point spark or star replaces the dot above the "i", filled in warm peach #E8A598. Tight, optically balanced kerning, refined and editorial, with a hint of tech modernity. Horizontal lockup, text only, nothing else. Looks premium on cream paper and also on a dark ink background.

**Tip:** this one needs the model to draw text correctly. If it misspells, rebuild it by hand in Canva or Figma with Instrument Serif.

## Concept 5: "Bold Badge" (confident and compact)

Works for stamps, leaflets, video end-cards and social profile pictures.

> A compact circular badge logo for Digital-tek. Inside a thick ink-navy #1A1F2E ring, a bold lowercase "dt" ligature, with the "d" in peach #F4C9B8 and the "t" in cream #FBF6EE, sharing a single stroke so they interlock. A tiny pixel-square detail at the top-right of the ring, in sky #B8D4E0. Strong, simple, symmetrical, high contrast, stamp-like but friendly. Flat vector, thick consistent line weights (about 6% of width), no thin hairlines so it survives printing at small sizes. Looks good as a 1 cm leaflet corner mark and as a 1080×1080 profile picture.

**With text:** add "Around the ring, small caps text 'DIGITAL-TEK · AI · WEB · CREATIVE' in DM Sans medium, evenly spaced."

## Sizes to request or export

| Use | Size | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Master file | 4096×4096 px (square) | PNG with transparent background; ask for SVG if the tool supports it |
| Website header | Horizontal lockup, about 600×160 px | SVG or PNG |
| Favicon / app icon | 512×512, 192×192, 32×32 px | PNG (and SVG) |
| Social profile pictures | 1080×1080 px | PNG |
| Leaflet / print | Vector (SVG or PDF) | Needed for sharp printing |
| Colour variants | Full colour, black, white (reversed), on ink navy | PNG or SVG |

## How to choose

Check each result against these tests:

1. **Small test:** shrink it to 32 px. Is it still recognisable?
2. **One-colour test:** does it work in pure black on white?
3. **Squint test:** can you tell what it is at arm's length?
4. **Fit test:** does it suit all five services (automation, web, SEO, content, creative) and sit well next to the cream-and-peach site?
5. **Spelling test:** is "Digital-tek" spelled correctly, with no stray letters?

**Practical suggestion:** try Concept 1 and Concept 4 together. A simple "d" mark with a serif wordmark is the easiest to apply consistently across leaflets, video end-cards and the website.

## Before you commit

- AI tools can unknowingly echo familiar designs. Run a reverse image search on the finished logo.
- Search the UK Intellectual Property Office trade mark register (gov.uk) to check it isn't too close to an existing mark.
- Check the licence terms of the AI tool you used, to confirm you can use its output commercially.
