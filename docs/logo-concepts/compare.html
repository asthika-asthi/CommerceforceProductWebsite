How to give feedbackPick your favourite and your second choice, and note anything you’d change (colour, shape, weight, lettering). Please look at the small sizes too: a logo has to work as a tiny browser icon.

Questions to think about

  1. Which one feels most like a company that builds automation, websites and creative work?
  2. Which one is still clear when it is tiny?
